# T1 relevance annotation guideline (summary)

Label 1 (market-relevant) if the article concerns any of:
- Macroeconomic policy: central-bank decisions, interest and exchange rates,
  taxes, foreign-ownership rules, fiscal policy.
- Listed companies: financial results, share issuance, dividends, M&A,
  restructuring, leadership changes, insider transactions.
- Market structure and trading: indices, liquidity, regulation.

Label 0 otherwise (lifestyle, society, sports, international stories with no
plausible transmission channel to Vietnamese equities).

Labels were first assigned by a commercial LLM following this guideline and then
reviewed in full by a human annotator with Vietnamese-market domain experience.
